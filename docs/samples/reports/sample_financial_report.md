# Demo Financial Research Report: Tesla 2025 Overview

## Company Overview
Tesla is an electric vehicle and energy company. Its main business includes vehicle sales, energy storage, software services, and autonomous driving-related products.

## Revenue Drivers
The major revenue drivers include vehicle deliveries, average selling price, energy storage growth, and software-related services.

## Key Opportunities
1. Expansion of energy storage business.
2. Growth potential in autonomous driving software.
3. Cost reduction through manufacturing efficiency.
4. International market expansion.

## Key Risks
1. EV market competition may reduce pricing power.
2. Regulatory uncertainty around autonomous driving.
3. Supply chain volatility.
4. Margin pressure from price cuts.

## Financial Indicators
Revenue growth remains an important indicator. Gross margin, operating margin, free cash flow, and R&D spending are also key metrics.

## Analyst View
The company has long-term growth potential, but valuation risk and execution risk should be carefully considered.
